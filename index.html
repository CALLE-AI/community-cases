HungryCall case

How HungryCall works

You define the conditions once. HungryCall checks candidates in order and stops after one restaurant confirms a match.

01

Set the conditions

You enter the location, food preference, delivery requirement, and maximum total price.

02

Call candidates in order

CALL-E asks one restaurant at a time about delivery, total price, and estimated arrival.

03

Stop on a match

HungryCall ends the sequence after one restaurant meets the rules, so it does not create a duplicate order.

Reusable pattern

A ranked calling cascade

Rank candidates, define the hard conditions, call one at a time, and stop after a confirmed match. The pattern also fits restaurant reservations, appointment searches, and local supplier checks.

Test record

Two author-run live sessions

The author routed each call to one consenting test number and played the restaurant roles. The public demo uses saved responses and cannot place calls.
