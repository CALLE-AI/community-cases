Build around clear rules.

Use this when one successful candidate is enough: restaurant reservations, appointment searches or local supplier checks. Keep the candidates, conditions and reasons for continuing visible to the person making the request.

  • Define the requirements. State what must be true, such as delivery to the requested address.
  • Set a hard boundary. Use a maximum total price or another explicit limit.
  • Keep the outcome. Record the result and why a candidate was accepted or rejected.
  • Review ambiguous outcomes. An uncertain agreement needs review before another business is called.

To place real calls, run and configure your own deployment using the author’s setup guide. The simulated demo lets you explore the decisions; it does not place an order.
