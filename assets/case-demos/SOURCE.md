# Community workflow demonstrations

These four interactive scenes, native controls and fictional fixtures are original CALL-E editorial implementations of the workflows already documented in the case guides. They are not recordings or replicas of the authors’ applications. No application code or design asset from proprietary Care Call AI is redistributed. The individual guides retain their author links, licensing information and known limitations; those limitations still apply to the actual applications. The simulations use deliberately simple, deterministic choices, not the authors’ detection or answer-validation algorithms.

The native scenes reuse the site's official vendored Three.js 0.180.0 modules; see [vendor source](../local-atlas-demo/vendor/SOURCE.md) and its retained MIT license. [scene-kit.mjs](../scene-kit.mjs) contains the characters, fixed-length phone rig, scene primitives, materials and renderer extracted from RingBack. It imports the existing `ik2.mjs` and `gait.mjs`; their upstream commits, adaptations and MIT notices remain in [RingBack motion sources](../ringback-demo/ADAPTATION.md), [LICENSE-HUASHU](../ringback-demo/LICENSE-HUASHU) and [LICENSE-PEOPLE](../ringback-demo/LICENSE-PEOPLE). English text is native HTML. All shown people, messages, requests, restaurant responses and bookings are fictional; state is page-local and makes no calls or external submissions.

The Conversation-Clarify, Care Call AI and HungryCall audio tracks were generated on 28 October 2026 with Microsoft Edge online TTS, voice `en-US-JennyNeural`, using `edge-tts` 7.3.1 and the upstream Lemo `tts_zh.py` helper bundled with Claude Motion. Each companion JSON retains the unchanged script, voice parameters, original segment timing and source/final hashes. Only the fictional narration text was sent for synthesis; the website serves local MP3 files without calling a speech service. The tracks now appear as optional standalone overviews under “Listen to the short overview,” with native audio controls and the script. They describe the default examples and are not synchronized with interactive branches. Sound requires an explicit action; captions and scripts remain available without audio or WebGL. The unused legacy RingBack audio retains its original ECNU provenance.

The geometry, responsive camera framing and choreography for Conversation-Clarify, Care Call AI and HungryCall are in `stories.mjs`; `scene.mjs` mounts each story with the shared renderer. The renderer retains native device pixel ratio, lighting and shadows, responsive sizing and WebGL context recovery. `data.mjs` provides immutable workflow transitions and fictional fixtures; `demo.mjs` runs manual choices and idle defaults through the same transitions. Input resets only the idle countdown. Pause auto stops automatic progression, Start over resets the workflow, and offscreen or hidden pages stop animation and progression. Reduced motion uses settled poses with auto initially paused; keyboard focus and the native controls remain available in WebGL fallback.

Each case has its own space and actions. Conversation-Clarify moves between the office desk and recipient, then returns a supported answer to an unsent draft; an unclear reply creates no draft. Care Call AI retains cleaning and groceries in separate clipboard slots before handing the whole record to the coordinator; an unclear callback retains cleaning and flags follow-up. HungryCall searches a restaurant street in order using a $1–$100 budget and required delivery, stops at the first match, or returns no match after four responses. It never places an order. Captions occupy normal document flow above the canvas, and the native panels use email, clipboard and receipt layouts. At 900px and below, a 360px canvas sits above the controls. The official CALL-E SVG and simulated call count appear in the panel footer, not on a 3D relay or moving record.

The original audio assembly used five speech segments for Conversation-Clarify and HungryCall and four for Care Call AI. Silence was padded to the original scene cues, with assertions that speech fit each interval; no spoken words were truncated or time-stretched. Local speech recognition verified those final transcripts at generation time. The retained JSON timing is provenance for these files, not a runtime animation clock; the interactive pages have no narration timing module or chapter-seeking player. Local Atlas retains its own narrated player and separate public-facts mock.

RingBack retains `../ringback-demo/scene.mjs` for its salon, rooms and choreography and `../ringback-demo/state.mjs` / `../ringback-demo/app.mjs` for its unified interactive flow, now using the same scene kit. It loads no narration track or separate mock. Its fictional diary screen is an original explanation, not a replica of the author's application; CALL-E remains in its native call and waiting-list controls.

Scene checks sample branch states and phase ages in desktop and narrow layouts, checking deterministic reconstruction, reduced-motion poses, phone alignment, moving records against faces, camera bounds and panel clearance. Browser checks cover default and uncertain branches, reset, keyboard focus, reduced motion and context-loss fallback. These local checks do not establish physical-phone performance, cross-browser coverage, real application behavior, user acceptance or deployment.
