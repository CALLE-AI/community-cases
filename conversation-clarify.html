Conversation-Clarify

Settle one missing detail. An email reply can sound like agreement while leaving a question open. Conversation-Clarify proposes one focused phone question, then prepares a draft for you to review.

Built by apple171 with CALL-E. Most Innovative · CALL-E Hackathon.

A Chrome extension and Python server. Try the local demo with saved responses, then review and send the draft yourself.

Workflow Example Try it

Find the unanswered part.

The extension reads the open Gmail conversation, identifies an unsettled choice or date and shows the question before any call.

  1. 1

    Inspect the finding

    Review the email evidence, proposed question, caller script and masked number. The number comes from the thread or from you.

  2. 2

    Approve one call

    Confirm the destination and question. The server sends the CALL-E request and polls for its result; the API credential stays on the server.

  3. 3

    Review the draft

    The app checks the answer and supporting words before drafting. You decide whether the result settles the question and how to reply.

From an unclear reply to a draft.

Illustrative email and phone exchange. You review the draft before sending.

Question
Would you prefer the morning or afternoon visit?
Email reply
“That sounds fine.” You still need a time.
Phone answer
“Afternoon, please.” The person chooses one option.
Draft to review
“Thanks for confirming the afternoon visit.”

Copy the draft into the original thread and check the recipient. The app’s new-compose shortcut does not carry over that thread or recipient.

Explore five possible responses.

The local demo includes resolved, voicemail, no answer, unresolved and an answer outside the offered options. It runs without a Gmail account and places no calls.

  • Preview before dialing. Show the exact question and destination together.
  • Keep unresolved results visible. A completed call can still leave the original question unanswered.
  • Review the words. The app can draft from an ambiguous answer, including a negated option or two possible days. Compare the answer and quote with the choices before using it.
  • Let the person send. A draft is an intermediate result, ready for review.

Explore the application and companion skill. The app carries an MIT license. Detection covers selected email patterns and depends on Gmail’s page structure.
